Design rules in these mocks

  • One switch — Codebase reverse ↔ Website reverse on the home form. No separate “deep” product surface in the hero.
  • Gone from home — Manual control checkbox, Deep Reverse upsell, hub→reverse tip clutter.
  • Cross-sell — “Try website reverse” / “Try codebase reversing” instead of “Want more depth? Deep Reverse”.
  • Website examples — YouTube, Pinterest, Xbox, Apple, Discord.
  • Codebase examples — Next.js, React, Supabase (kept familiar).
